## 📝 About Khokhar Wooden Experts

Founded on traditional joinery principles passed down through generations, **Khokhar Art & Craft** specializes in transforming raw **Sheesham (Indian Rosewood)**, **Burma Teak**, **Walnut**, and **Deodar** into architectural masterpieces.

Every slab of wood tells a story — from mortise, tenon, dovetail joints to hand-chiseled motifs, each piece is executed with surgical precision, seasoned against warping, and finished with organic protective oils.
