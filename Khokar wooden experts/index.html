Heritage & Craftmanship

The Legacy of Fine Joinery

پچیس سال سے لکڑی کے فن میں صداقت اور معیار کا ضامن

Uncompromising Passion for Real Timber

At Khokhar wooden Art & Craft, every slab of wood tells a story. Founded on traditional joinery principles passed down through generations, we specialize in transforming raw Sheesham (Indian Rosewood), Burma Teak, Walnut, and Deodar into architectural masterpieces.

We decline mass-produced synthetic composites. Every mortise, tenon, dovetail, and hand-chiseled motif is executed with surgical precision, seasoned against warping, and finished with organic protective oils.

Mastery Steps

Our Crafting Process

لکڑی کے شاہکار کی تیاری کے مراحل

01

Timber Seasoning

لکڑی کی سلیکشن اور خشکائی

Hand-selecting top-grade raw logs of Sheesham, Teak, or Deodar. Moisture level is tested and reduced below 10% to guarantee zero shrinkage or warping over decades.

02

Precision Joinery

روایتی جوڑ اور ڈیزائننگ

Drafting architectural blueprints followed by traditional interlocking joinery (Mortise & Tenon, Dovetail joints) providing structural durability without relying on cheap hardware.

03

Hand Carving & Polish

ہاتھ کی تراش خراش اور پالش

Our master artisans hand-chisel floral or geometric motifs. Finished with multi-stage hand-rubbed lacquers, PU sealer, or high-grade natural wax polish.

04

On-Site Fitting

مکمل انسٹالیشن اور فٹنگ

Carefully transported and installed at your home by our senior craftsmen with zero gap tolerance, seamless lock fittings, and final touch-up polish.
